Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026
The short version: Noctra has no server. Everything you enter is stored using your browser's built-in local storage, on your device only. Nothing is transmitted, collected, sold, or seen by anyone else — including us.

1. What Noctra is

Noctra is a free, client-side sleep-tracking website. "Client-side" means the entire application — the sleep score calculations, your history, your profiles — runs in your web browser. There is no backend database and no company server that Noctra sends your information to.

2. What information is stored, and where

When you create a local profile or log a night of sleep, the following may be stored in your browser's localStorage:

  • A display name you choose for your local profile
  • An optional 4–6 digit PIN, stored in plain text in your browser only (see Section 5 — this is not secure encryption)
  • Sleep logs: bedtime, wake time, a self-reported rest rating, or motion-sensor movement counts if you use live tracking
  • Calculated sleep scores derived from the above

This data never leaves your device. It is not sent over the network, not logged on any server, and not accessible to Noctra's creators, because there is nothing on our end receiving it.

3. Device motion data

If you use the "Track live" feature, Noctra requests permission to read your device's motion sensor (accelerometer) while tracking is active, to estimate how much you moved during the night. This data is processed entirely in your browser to compute a movement count — the raw sensor readings are never stored or transmitted, only the resulting summary (movements per hour) is saved locally alongside that night's log.

4. Cookies, analytics, and tracking

Noctra does not use cookies, third-party analytics, advertising trackers, or fingerprinting of any kind. The only network requests the page makes are to load its fonts from Google Fonts, which is standard for most websites and does not receive any of your sleep data.

5. Local PIN is not real security

The optional PIN on a local profile exists only to prevent someone glancing at a shared computer from accidentally opening the wrong person's data. It is not encrypted, not hashed, and not a substitute for real authentication. Anyone with direct access to your browser's storage (or basic technical knowledge) could bypass it. Do not use Noctra to store anything you consider truly sensitive.

6. Your control over your data

  • Export: download a full copy of your profile and logs as a JSON file at any time from the Profile section.
  • Delete: permanently delete a profile and all associated logs at any time. Since there is no server copy, this deletion is final and immediate.
  • Clearing your browser data (cache/site data) will also permanently remove everything Noctra has stored.

7. Not a medical device

Sleep scores, tiers, and movement-based estimates are simplified, self-reported or sensor-approximated metrics intended for personal insight and motivation only. They are not derived from clinical-grade sensors, are not a diagnosis, and should not be used to make medical decisions. If you have concerns about your sleep, please consult a licensed healthcare professional.

8. Children's privacy

Noctra does not knowingly collect information from children, largely because it does not collect information from anyone — all data stays local to the device it was entered on.

9. Changes to this policy

If this page is updated, the "Last updated" date above will change. Since Noctra has no accounts or emails on file, we have no way to notify you directly — please check back if you have concerns.

10. Contact

This is an independent, non-commercial project with no company, support team, or data-collection business model behind it.
