General guidance

A sample wind-down timeline

Evidence-based sleep hygiene steps — general guidance, not personalized to your data (that would need far more sensor input than a browser can honestly provide).

📵

Last screen

~90 min before bed

Blue light delays melatonin onset by roughly 2–3 hours for most people.

📖

Read to wind down

~45 min before bed

Low-arousal activity instead of screens tends to speed up sleep onset.

🌬

Slow breathing / meditate

~15–25 min before bed

Slow breathing is associated with lower cortisol and an easier transition to sleep.

🌙

Consistent sleep window

Same time nightly

A steady bed/wake time is one of the best-supported levers for sleep quality — track it in the history grid above.

Legal & data — plain language

What actually happens to your data

The short version below; full Privacy Policy and Terms of Service are linked in the footer.

Nothing leaves your browser

Sleep logs, scores, and profiles are stored using your browser's local storage. Noctra has no server and never transmits your data.

Not a medical device

Scores are estimates for personal insight only, not a diagnosis. Talk to a clinician about real sleep problems.

You control deletion

Delete a profile any time to permanently erase its data from this device — there's no copy anywhere else to ask us to remove.

FAQ

Good to know

Do I need a smartwatch?+

No. Log manually, or use the live phone-motion tracker. Wearable integrations aren't supported — those require paid manufacturer partnerships, which is exactly the kind of thing that turns a free app into a subscription.

How is the score calculated?+

Mostly duration versus an 8-hour target, plus how rested you said you felt (manual) or how much movement was detected (live tracking). It's a useful estimate, not a lab-grade metric.

Will my data sync across my devices?+

No — that needs a server and accounts, which is the tradeoff for staying free with nothing to monetize. Use Export to save a backup file and keep your own copy.

Is the local PIN actually secure?+

No. It only separates profiles on a shared device — it is not encryption or real authentication. Don't rely on it to protect sensitive information.
